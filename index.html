The Dissertation

The Creative Production (i.e. project).

A creative production dissertation in three parts.

01 · The Question

What does data visualization reveal about Staley’s impact that traditional sports media obscures?

And how can visualization design challenge dominant narratives about women’s athletes?

02 · The Method

Critical making as inquiry — building the artifact is the research.

Literature review across four threads. Open-source data from Her Hoops Stats and Basketball-Reference.

03 · The Artifact

An interactive, born-digital web dashboard.

The visualization isn’t reporting the finding — it is the finding.

Theoretical foundations

Solid lines are named research areas. Dashed and dotted lines are the cross-cutting and methodological threads woven through both.

Kris E. Smith.

Doctoral student in Clemson University’s Rhetoric, Communication, and Information Design program. Her work sits at the intersection of data visualization and sports communication, with attention to how visual design can challenge dominant narratives about women’s basketball.
